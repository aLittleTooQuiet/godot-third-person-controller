A basic Godot third person controller with mouse/keyboard and controller support.

Uses the following actions in your Input Map:
`move_forward` - Forward/up on the move axis,
`move_back` - Backward/down on the move axis,
`move_left` - Left on the move axis,
`move_right` - Right on the move axis,
`camera_forward` - Forward/up on the camera axis,
`camera_backward` - Backward/down on the camera axis,
`camera_left` - Left on the camera axis,
`camera_right` - Right on the camera axis,
